Yes. Every AX harness features our Rapid-Slide torso adjustment system that calibrates smoothly for frames from 5'0" (152cm) to 6'5" (196cm), making our 15L–30L packs and slings ideal for both boys and adult men.

How does the Lifetime Ironclad Warranty work?

If a zipper, buckle, seam, or 1680D ballistic panel ever fails under normal or athletic use, we repair or replace your bag free of charge at any of our global service centers.
